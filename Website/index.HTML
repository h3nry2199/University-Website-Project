Festival Overview

MusicFest 2025 is the ultimate celebration of music, bringing together major artists and fans from around the world for an unforgettable experience. Join us for two days of live performances, food, and fun!

The date of this awesome event is 30th - 31st December 2025 at 7pm - 3am, taking place at The University of Leicester (see the map attached).

Festival Theme

Expect a diverse lineup of artists spanning multiple genres, from rock and pop to hip-hop and electronic music. If you asking yourself "What can I wear ?", then heres you answer, to fit the theme of the festival choose any bright and colourful clothing with a funky twist !
