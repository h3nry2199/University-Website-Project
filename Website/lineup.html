Artist Lineup

Meet the performers at MusicFest 2025.

Arctic Monkeys are an English rock band formed in Sheffield in 2002. The band consists of Alex Turner, Jamie Cook, Nick O'Malley, and Matt Helders. Known for their energetic performances and catchy tunes, they have released several critically acclaimed albums.

Drake is a Canadian rapper, singer, actor, and businessman who rose to fame as global music superstar known for blending hip-hop with R&B, topping charts with hits like "God's Plan" and "Hotline Bling," founding OVO Sound, and winning multiple Grammys. He's one of the best-selling artists ever, celebrated for his emotional lyrics and chart dominance, becoming a cultural icon and successful entrepreneur.

Fleetwood Mac are a British-American rock band formed in London in 1967 by singer and guitarist Peter Green. Green named the band by combining the surnames of drummer Mick Fleetwood and bassist John McVie, who have remained with the band throughout its many line-up changes. Fleetwood Mac have sold more than 120 million records worldwide, making them one of the world's best-selling musical acts.
